🏠 BARODA HOME LOAN
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Standard home loan for purchase, construction, extension, repair, and renovation of residential property.

Interest Rate:  
Starting from 7.50% p.a. (floating) – subject to change and applicant profile

Processing Fee:  
As per Bank’s schedule (refer branch/brochure)

Tenure:  
Up to 30 years or up to retirement age as per policy

Loan Amount Range:  
Up to ₹20 Crore (subject to location and borrower profile)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indians & NRIs (specific NRI variant available)  
• Age: 18 – 70 years (at loan maturity)  
• Income: Salaried / Self-employed with valid income proof  

Documents Required:  
• ID proof  
• Address proof  
• Income proof (payslips / Form16 / ITR)  
• Property documents  
• Bank statements  

Features:  
• Daily reducing balance interest calculation  
• No prepayment charges on floating rate loans  
• Top-up & balance transfer facilities available  
• Concession on interest rate for women borrowers

Profile-based, often lower than previous lender’s rate

Processing Fee:  
As per Bank’s balance transfer schedule; includes legal & valuation charges

Tenure:  
Remaining tenure of existing loan or extended as per eligibility

Loan Amount Range:  
Equivalent to outstanding balance + top-up (if eligible)

Eligibility:

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------------
Q: What is the tenure range for Baroda Home Loans?  
A: Up to 30 years or until retirement age; minimum usually 5 years.  

Q: Can NRIs apply?  
A: Yes, NRI variants are available with special documentation norms.  

Q: Are prepayments allowed?  
A: Yes. Floating rate loans have zero prepayment penalty; fixed rates may differ.  

Q: What is a home loan takeover?  
A: Transfer of an existing home loan from another lender to Bank of Baroda for better terms.  

Q: Are there women borrower concessions?  
A: Yes, female applicants may get reduced interest rates.  

Q: What documents are needed for valuation?  
A: Sale agreement, title deeds, approved plan, occupancy certificate (if applicable).  

Q: How is loan amount decided?  
A: Based on income, property value, age, and internal credit policy.  

Q: Can I apply online?  
A: Yes — apply directly via the Bank of Baroda website.  

Q: Is home insurance required?  
A: Yes — property insurance is generally mandatory before disbursement.  

Q: What is a pre-approved home loan?  
A: A sanction given before property selection, valid for a fixed period.  

Q: How long does disbursal take?  
A: Typically 5–15 working days depending on documentation and valuation.  

Q: Can I get a top-up loan?  
A: Yes, based on repayment record and equity in property.  

Q: What are processing charges?  
A: Processing fee, valuation, legal, and stamp duty as per Bank’s schedule.  

Q: Is stage-wise disbursement available for construction loans?  
A: Yes — funds released in stages upon builder certification.  

Q: What’s the maximum loan amount?  
A: Up to ₹20 Crore depending on city, income, and LTV ratio.  

Q: Are tax benefits available?  
A: Yes — as per Income Tax Act Sections 80C and 24(b).  

Q: Can co-applicants be added?  
A: Yes — spouse or family members can apply jointly to enhance eligibility.  

Q: How is interest calculated?  
A: On daily reducing balance basis.
