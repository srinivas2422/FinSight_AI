🎓 BARODA SCHOLAR (FOR ABROAD STUDIES)
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Education loan to finance higher education abroad (undergraduate, postgraduate, doctoral, or professional programmes) with tailored features for premier and non-premier institutions.

Interest Rate:  
7.65% – 12.50% p.a. (indicative; varies by borrower profile & institution)

Processing Fee:  
No processing/documentation charges for certain slabs; nominal fee (e.g., 1% refundable) for higher loan amounts — confirm at branch.

Tenure:  
Repayment tenure up to 10–15 years after moratorium (course + grace period)

Loan Amount Range:  
• Up to ₹150 Lakh for premier universities abroad  
• Up to ₹60 Lakh for other institutions  

Eligibility:  
• Indian nationals (NRIs may be covered in select variants)  
• Co-applicant required (parent/guardian)  
• Age: Student as per course; co-applicant per bank norms  
• Course Type: Professional / Technical / Management / Medical / Doctoral abroad  

Documents Required:  
• KYC of applicant & co-applicant  
• Admission/offer letter from recognised foreign institution  
• Cost-of-study breakup / fee schedule  
• Academic records (marksheets, certificates)  
• Income proof of co-applicant (salary slips/Form16/ITR)  
• Passport & visa copy (where applicable)  

Features:  
• High limits for premier universities  
• Moratorium during study + grace period  
• Nil/nominal processing fee  
• Funds for tuition & living expenses abroad  
• Flexible repayment & top-up facilities

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------------
Q: Who can apply for a Bank of Baroda education loan?  
A: Students (Indian/NRI) with confirmed admission to recognised courses; co-applicant required.

Q: What is the ‘Baroda Scholar’ scheme?  
A: Flagship loan for studies abroad with limits up to ₹150 Lakh for top universities.

Q: Are vocational and skill loans available?  
A: Yes — Baroda Skill Loan and similar digital variants.

Q: What is the moratorium period?  
A: Course duration + 6–12 months (varies by scheme).

Q: Do all loans need collateral?  
A: Only above certain limits or for non-premier institutes; small loans often unsecured.

Q: Are living expenses abroad covered?  
A: Yes — tuition, hostel, travel, visa, and living costs included in sanctioned amount.

Q: What documents are needed?  
A: Admission letter, KYC, academic proof, cost estimates, co-applicant income proof, passport/visa.

Q: What about processing fees?  
A: Nil for smaller slabs; nominal refundable fee for higher limits.

Q: How is loan amount decided?  
A: Based on cost, security, credit profile, and repayment capacity.

Q: Is top-up possible?  
A: Yes, subject to eligibility and repayment track record.

Q: Are there concessions for premier institutes?  
A: Yes — lower interest & higher loan caps.

Q: How long does disbursement take?  
A: 1–3 days for small digital loans; larger loans may take longer.

Q: Can NRIs/PIOs apply?  
A: Yes — specific variants exist with separate documentation norms.

Q: What happens if I default?  
A: Penal charges, recovery action, and impact on credit history.

Q: Are there linked government subsidy schemes?  
A: Yes — Vidyalaxmi, interest subsidy programs, and others may apply.

Q: Are tax benefits available?  
A: Yes — under Section 80E of the Income Tax Act for interest paid.

Q: Can I apply online?  
A: Yes — fully digital application available on Bank’s website.

Q: Is foreign currency disbursal allowed?  
A: Usually in INR to institutions; some cases may involve remittance options.

Q: What about executive programs?  
A: Support up to ₹80 Lakh or more for select institutions.

Q: Are scholarships factored in?  
A: Yes — they reduce the sanctioned amount required.

Q: Can I prepay early?  
A: Yes — most loans allow prepayment without penalty.
