Up to 84 months (7 years)

Loan Amount Range:  
Up to ₹5 crore (based on eligibility)

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------------
Q: What is the maximum finance available for a new car?  
A: Up to 90% of on-road price depending on profile & eligibility.  

Q: Can I get a used car loan?  
A: Yes, for certified pre-owned cars from approved dealers.  

Q: What is the typical tenure for car loans?  
A: Up to 84 months for new cars; 60 months for used cars.  

Q: Is vehicle insurance mandatory?  
A: Yes, comprehensive insurance is required before disbursal.  

Q: Are NRI applicants eligible?  
A: Yes, under specific NRI/PIO variants — check bank policy.  

Q: What documents are required?  
A: ID, address, income proof, dealer invoice, photographs, and KYC.  

Q: Is prepayment allowed?  
A: Yes; foreclosure rules depend on product. Floating rate loans often have no penalty.  

Q: How fast is loan disbursal?  
A: Digital offers within 24–48 hours; manual processing takes 3–5 working days.  

Q: Can farmers apply for two-wheeler loans?  
A: Yes, under eligible categories of the two-wheeler scheme.  

Q: What is margin/margin money?  
A: It’s the customer’s contribution to the vehicle price. Percentage varies per vehicle type.  

Q: Does BoB offer commercial vehicle loans?  
A: Yes, under MSME/business banking vertical.  

Q: Can I get a top-up loan?  
A: Top-ups may be offered to eligible customers.  

Q: Are co-applicants allowed?  
A: Yes, family members or guarantors may apply jointly.  

Q: How is EMI calculated?  
A: Based on principal, interest rate, and tenure using reducing balance.  

Q: What if I miss an EMI?  
A: Penal charges apply, and credit score may be affected.
