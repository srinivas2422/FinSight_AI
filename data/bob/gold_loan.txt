📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------------
Q: Who can apply for a Baroda gold loan?  
A: Any individual (resident or eligible NRI) owning gold jewellery/coins meeting bank purity norms.  

Q: What is the maximum LTV offered?  
A: LTV depends on bank & RBI policy; typically up to regulatory ceiling based on gold market value.  

Q: What’s the usual tenure?  
A: 3 to 12 months; some products allow up to 36 months with renewals.  

Q: Is pledged gold insured?  
A: Yes, most schemes include insurance coverage for pledged items.  

Q: Are prepayment charges applicable?  
A: Usually nil for retail gold loans; confirm per product.  

Q: Can I extend or renew my gold loan?  
A: Yes, renewal is allowed on repayment of interest as per bank norms.  

Q: How fast is the disbursal?  
A: Same-day disbursal after valuation and documentation completion.  

Q: What gold purity is accepted?  
A: Generally 18K to 22K jewellery subject to valuation and verification.  

Q: Can companies avail gold loans?  
A: Yes, via MSME/business gold loan variants.  

Q: What documents are required?  
A: KYC, PAN (if applicable), jewellery valuation details, and pledge form.  

Q: Is there a maximum limit per borrower?  
A: Up to ₹50 lakh (retail); varies for MSME loans.  

Q: Can pledged gold under lien be used?  
A: No, gold must be free from any lien or prior loan.  

Q: How is interest charged?  
A: Monthly/fortnightly on outstanding principal as per sanction terms.  

Q: Are digital valuation options available?  
A: Yes — available under Baroda Digital Gold Loan.  

Q: What happens in case of default?  
A: Bank issues statutory notices and may auction gold after due period as per RBI norms.
