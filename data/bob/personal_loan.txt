📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------------
Q: Who is eligible for a Baroda personal loan?  
A: Salaried, self-employed and professionals meeting income, age, and credit criteria.  

Q: Can I get a loan without collateral?  
A: Yes, Baroda personal loans are unsecured.  

Q: What is the processing time?  
A: Digital or pre-approved loans can be same-day; standard loans take a few working days post-documentation.  

Q: What is the maximum tenure?  
A: Up to 60–84 months, depending on product and profile.  

Q: Are co-applicants allowed?  
A: Varies by product; some allow guarantors, others do not.  

Q: Can I pre-pay or foreclose?  
A: Yes, subject to product-specific conditions. Many have zero foreclosure charges.  

Q: What documents are required?  
A: ID proof, address proof, salary slips/Form16/ITR, bank statements, and photos.  

Q: Are there pre-approved offers?  
A: Yes, select customers get instant pre-approved offers.  

Q: Is there a salary account benefit?  
A: Yes, salary account holders enjoy faster processing and concessional charges.  

Q: Can NRIs apply?  
A: Specific NRI personal loan products may exist; refer to NRI loan section.  

Q: What factors decide the interest rate?  
A: Credit score, income, employment stability, and customer relationship.  

Q: Is digital application available?  
A: Yes, through Baroda’s online channels and mobile banking app.  

Q: What’s the minimum loan amount?  
A: Usually ₹50,000.  

Q: Are there offers for women?  
A: Occasionally, special rate concessions may apply.  

Q: How is EMI calculated?  
A: Based on loan amount, tenure, and rate; online EMI calculator available.  

Q: Can I get a top-up loan?  
A: Yes, eligible borrowers can avail top-up after satisfactory repayment record.  

Q: What happens if I miss an EMI?  
A: Late payment penalties apply, and credit score impact may occur.
