🎓 EDUCATION LOAN FOR INDIAN EDUCATION
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Term loan for Indian residents pursuing higher education in India, covering tuition fees, books, living expenses, and equipment costs at recognised institutions.

Interest Rate:  
• Starting from ~10.50% p.a. onwards  

Processing Fee:  
• Nil for loans up to ₹7.5 lakh  
• Above ₹7.5 lakh – approx 1% of loan amount  

Tenure:  
• Up to 15 years including moratorium (course period + 6–12 months)

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: Need-based (small amounts accepted)  
• Maximum: Up to ₹150 lakh (loans up to ₹50 lakh without collateral for select institutes)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Indian resident  
• Minimum Age: 16 years  
• Maximum Age: 35 years (student applicant)

Documents Required:  
• Completed loan application form with photographs  
• Proof of identity & address (applicant and co-applicant)  
• Academic records & admission letter from recognised institution  
• Fee schedule from the institution  
• Co-applicant mandatory (parent/guardian) for full-time courses

🌍 EDUCATION LOAN FOR FOREIGN (OVERSEAS) EDUCATION
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Loan for Indian students pursuing education abroad covering tuition, travel, living, and other study-related costs.

Interest Rate:  
• From ~8.64% p.a. (secured/strong profile) upwards  

Processing Fee:  
• Up to 1% of loan amount (approximate)

Tenure:  
• Up to 15 years including moratorium  

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: As per course cost  
• Maximum: Up to ₹45 lakh unsecured; no upper cap with collateral  

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Indian resident  
• Minimum Age: 16 years  
• Maximum Age: 35 years (student applicant)  

Documents Required:  
• Loan application form with photographs  
• Proof of identity & address (applicant and co-applicant)  
• Admission/offer letter from overseas institution  
• Schedule of expenses (tuition, travel, living)  
• Collateral documentation (if applicable)

🏛️ CSIS – CENTRAL GOVERNMENT INTEREST SUBSIDY SCHEME
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Government subsidy scheme for students from economically weaker sections, offering interest waiver on eligible portions of education loans.

Interest Rate:  
• As per subsidy – interest waived for eligible EWS students  

Processing Fee:  
• As per bank policy + statutory charges  

Tenure:  
• Up to 15 years including moratorium  

Loan Amount Range:  
• As per standard education loan (up to ₹150 lakh)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Indian resident (Economically Weaker Section)  
• Minimum Age: 16 years  
• Maximum Age: 35 years  

Documents Required:  
• Original income certificate (EWS category)  
• Loan application & education loan agreement  
• Bonafide/provisional certificate from institution  
• Standard KYC documents for applicant & co-applicant

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
-----------------------------------------
Q1: What is the interest rate for HDFC education loans?  
A: Starts from ~10.50% p.a for Indian education and ~8.64% p.a for secured/strong overseas profiles.

Q2: Is there a processing fee for HDFC education loans?  
A: Nil for loans up to ₹7.5 lakh; approx 1% of the loan amount for higher loans.

Q3: Do I need to provide collateral for an HDFC education loan?  
A: Not required up to ₹50 lakh for select institutes; required for larger or overseas loans.

Q4: What is the maximum tenure?  
A: Up to 15 years including moratorium.

Q5: What is the age eligibility?  
A: Students aged 16–35 years can apply.
