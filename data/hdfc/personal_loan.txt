💰 HDFC XPRESS PERSONAL LOAN
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Quick digital personal loan up to ₹40 lakh with minimal documentation and instant approval for eligible customers.

Interest Rate:  
• Starting from ~9.99% p.a. (for select offers)

Processing Fee:  
• Up to ~2.50% of loan amount (minimum & maximum thresholds apply)

Tenure:  
• Up to 60 months (5 years)

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: ₹50,000 (approx.)  
• Maximum: ₹40 lakh

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian salaried account holder  
• Minimum Age: 21 years  
• Maximum Age: 60 years (at application)

Documents Required:  
• Proof of identity (PAN/Aadhaar/Passport)  
• Proof of address (Utility bill/Aadhaar/Driving License)  
• Salary slips (last 3 months) or bank statements (last 3 months)

👩‍💼 HDFC PERSONAL LOAN FOR WOMEN
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Personal loan scheme for women borrowers to meet needs like travel, marriage, education, or emergencies.

Interest Rate:  
• 9.99% to 24.00% p.a. (fixed rate)

Processing Fee:  
• Up to ₹6,500 + GST (for non-preapproved applicants)

Tenure:  
• 3 months to 72 months (up to 6 years)

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: ₹50,000 (approx.)  
• Maximum: ₹40 lakh

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian woman applicant  
• Minimum Age: 21 years  
• Maximum Age: 60 years (at application)

Documents Required:  
• Proof of identity & address  
• Last 3 months bank statements or salary slips

💎 HDFC GOLDEN EDGE PERSONAL LOAN
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Premium personal loan for high-income customers (loan amount ≥ ₹15 lakh) offering best rates and zero foreclosure charges.

Interest Rate:  
• Starting from ~9.99% p.a. for eligible applicants

Processing Fee:  
• Flat ₹6,500 + GST

Tenure:  
• Up to 72 months (6 years)

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: ₹15 lakh  
• Maximum: ₹40 lakh (or higher for eligible customers)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian salaried / company directors  
• Minimum Age: 21 years  
• Maximum Age: 60 years (at application)

Documents Required:  
• Premium customer application form  
• Proof of identity & address (PAN)  
• Income proof (salary slips, Form 16, last 2 years)  
• Company ID / employment credentials (if applicable)

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
-----------------------------------------
Q1: What is the current interest rate for HDFC personal loans?  
A: Rates start from around 9.99% p.a., depending on applicant profile, amount, and tenure.

Q2: What processing fee is charged for personal loans?  
A: Up to 2.50% of the loan amount (for salaried) or flat ₹6,500 for premium variants.

Q3: What is the maximum tenure for HDFC personal loans?  
A: Up to 60 months (5 years) for most loan types.

Q4: Can I get a personal loan for a wedding or travel?  
A: Yes, HDFC offers dedicated personal loans for marriage, travel, or medical emergencies.

Q5: Are part-prepayment or foreclosure options available?  
A: Yes, both are allowed. Charges may apply based on loan variant and timing of closure.
