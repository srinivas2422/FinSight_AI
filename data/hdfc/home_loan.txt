-----------------------------------------
🏠 HDFC HOME LOAN – NEW PURCHASE / CONSTRUCTION
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Loan for purchase of new or ready-to-move home or construction of house on a plot.

Interest Rate:  
• Starting from ~8.50% p.a. onwards (depending on eligibility & borrower profile)

Processing Fee:  
• Up to ~0.50% of loan amount or ₹3,300 (whichever higher) + applicable charges

Tenure:  
• Up to 30 years (subject to borrower age & property eligibility)

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: Need-based (based on property value)  
• Maximum: Up to ₹10 crore (for eligible cases)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian (and other eligible categories)  
• Minimum Age: 21 years  
• Maximum Age: 65 years (for salaried/self-employed at application)

Documents Required:  
• Completed home loan application form with photographs  
• Proof of identity & address (PAN/Aadhaar/Passport/Driving License)  
• Income proof (salary slips/ITR/bank statements)  
• Property documents (Agreement, approved plan, occupancy certificate, etc.)

-----------------------------------------
🔁 HDFC HOME LOAN – BALANCE TRANSFER & TOP-UP
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Facility to transfer existing home loan from another bank to HDFC Bank for better interest rate, or to avail additional top-up funding.

Interest Rate:  
• Starting from ~8.15% p.a. onwards (for transfer/top-up cases)

Processing Fee:  
• Up to ~0.50% of loan amount (as per bank policy)

Tenure:  
• Remaining loan tenure or new tenure up to 30 years (as per eligibility)

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: Existing loan outstanding amount  
• Maximum: Based on eligibility and property valuation

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian (and other eligible applicants)  
• Minimum Age: 21 years  
• Maximum Age: 65 years (salaried/self-employed)

Documents Required:  
• Application form for balance transfer/top-up  
• Loan account statement from existing lender (last 6–12 months)  
• Property title documents & valuation report  
• Proof of identity, address & income  

-----------------------------------------
🏡 HDFC HOME LOAN – RENOVATION / EXTENSION / PLOT LOAN
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Loan for renovation, extension, improvement of existing home, or purchase of a residential plot for construction.

Interest Rate:  
• Similar to regular home loan rates (~8.50% p.a. onwards)

Processing Fee:  
• Up to ~0.50% of loan amount or minimum fixed charge (as per policy)

Tenure:  
• Up to 30 years (depending on borrower’s age and profile)

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: Based on renovation/improvement cost  
• Maximum: As per property valuation & eligibility

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian (and eligible categories)  
• Minimum Age: 21 years  
• Maximum Age: 65 years  

Documents Required:  
• Application form with photos  
• Proof of identity & address  
• Income proof (salary slips/ITR/bank statements)  
• Property documents (approved plan, renovation/extension estimate, plot papers, etc.)

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
-----------------------------------------
Q1: What is the current interest rate for HDFC home loans?  
A: HDFC offers home loans starting from around 8.50% p.a., based on borrower profile, property, and tenure.

Q2: What processing fee is charged for home loans?  
A: Up to ~0.50% of loan amount or a minimum ₹3,300 (whichever is higher).

Q3: What is the maximum tenure for HDFC home loans?  
A: Up to 30 years (depending on borrower age and property eligibility).

Q4: Can self-employed individuals apply?  
A: Yes, both salaried and self-employed individuals aged 21–65 years can apply.

Q5: Are there special offers for women or plot loans?  
A: Yes, HDFC provides variants such as plot, renovation, and extension loans, and may offer preferential rates for women borrowers.
