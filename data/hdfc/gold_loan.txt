🏦 HDFC GOLD LOAN – STANDARD TERM LOAN
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Loan against pledged gold jewellery or coins; repayable in EMIs or bullet/term repayment. Generally for personal or business needs.

Interest Rate:  
• ~9.30% p.a. to ~17.86% p.a. (depends on gold quality, loan amount & borrower profile)

Processing Fee:  
• Up to ~1% of loan amount (+GST applicable)

Tenure:  
• 6 months to 24–42 months depending on repayment option

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: ₹25,000 (rural areas approx ₹10,000)  
• Maximum: No fixed public limit; depends on gold value & branch norms

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian  
• Minimum Age: 18 years  
• Maximum Age: ~75 years

Documents Required:  
• Proof of Identity (PAN, Aadhaar, DL, Passport)  
• Proof of Address (Utility Bill, Aadhaar, DL)  
• Passport-size Photograph  
• Gold jewellery/coins meeting purity norms  

-----------------------------------------
💳 HDFC GOLD LOAN – OVERDRAFT FACILITY
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Gold loan variant offering overdraft facility – borrower pledges gold and can withdraw flexibly; interest payable only on amount utilised.

Interest Rate:  
• ~12% p.a. (indicative as per HDFC Bank page)

Processing Fee:  
• Up to ~1% of loan amount (standard gold loan charge)

Tenure:  
• 24–36 months depending on facility

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: ₹25,000  
• Maximum: Based on value of pledged gold (per LTV norms)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian  
• Minimum Age: 18 years  
• Maximum Age: ~75 years  

Documents Required:  
• Proof of Identity & Address  
• Passport-size Photograph  
• Gold jewellery/coins to pledge  

-----------------------------------------

-----------------------------------------
🏢 HDFC GOLD LOAN – BUSINESS / MSME GOLD LOAN
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Gold loan designed for MSMEs or business owners for working capital or expansion. May offer higher LTV and relaxed documentation.

Interest Rate:  
• 9.30%–17.86% p.a. (as per standard gold loan rates)

Processing Fee:  
• NIL for MSME loans up to ₹5 lakh with valid Udyam registration  
• Otherwise ~1% of loan amount

Tenure:  
• Typically up to 24–36 months  

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: As per gold value/business need  
• Maximum: ₹50 lakh+ possible depending on gold pledged  

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian (business owner / MSME)  
• Minimum Age: 18 years  
• Maximum Age: ~75 years  

Documents Required:  
• Proof of Identity & Address  
• Passport-size Photographs  
• MSME registration certificate (for waiver benefit)  
• Gold jewellery/coins pledged  

-----------------------------------------
📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
-----------------------------------------
Q1: What is the interest rate for HDFC gold loans?  
A: Rates range from ~9.30% p.a. to ~17.86% p.a. depending on scheme, gold purity, and borrower profile.

Q2: Is there a processing fee for HDFC gold loans?  
A: Yes, up to ~1% of the loan amount. MSME borrowers with Udyam registration may get NIL fee for loans up to ₹5 lakh.

Q3: What is the maximum tenure for HDFC gold loans?  
A: Generally up to 24 months (term/EMI), shorter for bullet loans (~6 months).

Q4: What is the minimum loan amount available?  
A: Typically ₹25,000 in urban areas; around ₹10,000 in rural regions.

Q5: What repayment modes are available?  
A: EMI mode, bullet repayment, overdraft facility, and MSME business variant.
