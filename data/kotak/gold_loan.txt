-----------------------------------------
🏦 KOTAK GOLD LOAN – REGULAR GOLD LOAN
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Loan against gold jewellery or coins (18–22 karat jewellery or up to 50 g bank-minted 24 karat coins) for personal or non-agricultural needs.

Interest Rate:  
Starting from ~9% p.a. upwards (indicative)

Processing Fee:  
Up to ~2% of loan amount + applicable GST

Tenure:  
• Bullet scheme: 12 months (renewable)  
• Vanilla scheme: up to 36 months  
• EMI scheme: up to 48 months  

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: ≈₹20,000 (approx)  
• Maximum: Up to ~₹1.5 crore (as per third-party summary)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian  
• Minimum Age: 18 years  
• Maximum Age: Up to ~75 years (approx)

Documents Required:  
• Proof of identity (PAN/Aadhaar/Driving Licence)  
• Proof of address (Utility bill/Aadhaar/Driving Licence)  
• Gold jewellery/coins to pledge (18–22 karat jewellery or specified coins)  
• Two/three passport-size photographs

-----------------------------------------

No processing fee (as per public page)

Tenure:

Loan Amount Range:

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian

• Maximum Age: Not specified  

Documents Required:  
• Gold jewellery/coins to pledge (18–22 karat jewellery or specified coins)

-----------------------------------------
📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
-----------------------------------------
Q1: What is the interest rate for Kotak gold loans?  
A: Interest rates start from ~9% p.a. for regular gold loans; Smart Choice scheme fixed at 0.88% per month (~10.56% p.a.).

Q2: What processing fee is charged on Kotak gold loans?  
A: Up to ~2% + GST for regular/EMI schemes; no processing fee under Smart Choice offer.

Q3: What tenures are available under Kotak gold loans?  
A: Bullet – 12 months (renewable), Vanilla – up to 36 months, EMI – up to 48 months.

Q4: Do I need to submit income documents?  
A: For loans under ₹25 lakh (Smart Choice), no income documents required; larger loans may need ITR/income proof.

Q5: What types of gold are accepted?  
A: Only 18–22 karat jewellery and 24 karat bank-minted coins (up to 50 g). Other items (e.g. idols, watches) not accepted.
