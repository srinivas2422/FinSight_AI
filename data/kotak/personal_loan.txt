💰 KOTAK PERSONAL LOAN – UP TO ₹35 LAKHS
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
A general-purpose personal loan for salaried individuals to meet needs such as travel, medical, home renovation, or debt consolidation.

Interest Rate:  
• Starting from 9.98% p.a. onward  

Processing Fee:  
• Up to 5% of the loan amount (non-refundable) + applicable taxes  

Tenure:  
• Up to 6 years (72 months)  

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: Around ₹50,000 (approx, not officially stated)  
• Maximum: Up to ₹35 Lakhs (subject to eligibility)  

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian (salaried)  
• Minimum Age: 21 years  
• Maximum Age: 60 years (salaried, at application)  

Documents Required:  
• Proof of identity (PAN/Aadhaar/Passport/Driving Licence)  
• Proof of residence (Utility bill/Aadhaar/Driving Licence)  
• Income proof – last 3 months’ salary slips or bank statements  
• Last 3 months’ bank account statement
