-----------------------------------------
🎓 KOTAK EDUCATION LOAN – DOMESTIC STUDIES
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Term loan for Indian nationals pursuing higher education in India, covering tuition fees and related study expenses.

Interest Rate:  
Starting from ~9% – 11% p.a. (secured)  
Up to ~16% p.a. (unsecured; indicative)

Processing Fee:  
Nil (no processing fee as per public listings)

Tenure:  
Up to 15 years including moratorium (course period + 12 months)

Loan Amount Range:  
Up to ₹10 lakh (for study in India)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Indian National  
• Minimum Age: Not specified for student; co-applicant must be ≥21 years  
• Maximum Age: Co-applicant up to ~70 years at loan maturity  

Documents Required:  
• Completed loan application form with passport-size photographs  
• Proof of identity & address (PAN/Aadhaar/Passport/Driving Licence)  
• Educational mark-sheets and qualifying certificates  
• Admission/offer letter from recognised Indian institution  
• Fee schedule from the institution  
• Income proof of co-applicant/guardian  

-----------------------------------------
🌍 KOTAK EDUCATION LOAN – OVERSEAS STUDIES (via Credila Tie-up)
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Loan for Indian students pursuing higher education abroad, covering tuition, travel, living, laptop, and study-related expenses via tie-up with Credila Financial Services.

Interest Rate:  
Starting from ~10.25% p.a. upwards (unsecured up to high amounts)  
Typically up to ~12%–14% p.a. (depending on collateral and profile)

Processing Fee:  
Up to ~1.25% of sanctioned amount + taxes (as per Credila’s terms)

Tenure:  
Up to 15 years (course + moratorium + repayment)

Loan Amount Range:  
• Regular overseas education: Up to ₹20 lakh  
• Tie-up / Premium cases (via Credila): Up to ₹75 lakh  

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Indian National  
• Minimum Age: None specified for student; co-applicant must be ≥21 years  
• Maximum Age: Co-applicant up to ~70 years at loan maturity  

Documents Required:  
• Completed loan application form with photos  
• Proof of identity & address (applicant & co-applicant)  
• Admission/offer letter from overseas university  
• Detailed cost sheet/travel/living expense estimate  
• Passport & visa (if applicable)  
• Collateral documents if required (for large loans)  
• Income proof of co-applicant/guardian  

-----------------------------------------
📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
-----------------------------------------
Q1: What loan amount can I avail under Kotak education loan?  
A: Up to ₹10 lakh for studies in India; up to ₹20 lakh for abroad; in tie-up (Credila) cases up to ₹75 lakh.

Q2: Is there a processing fee for Kotak education loans?  
A: No processing fee for standard Kotak education loans; for tie-up overseas cases, up to ~1.25% of sanctioned amount applies.

Q3: What is the tenure for Kotak education loans?  
A: Up to 15 years (including course period and moratorium) for both domestic and overseas education loans.

Q4: Is age limit applicable for Kotak education loans?  
A: No specific upper limit for the student; co-applicant must be ≥21 years at origination and ≤70 years at maturity.

Q5: What expenses are covered under Kotak education loans?  
A: Tuition fees, hostel/accommodation, books, laptop/computer, travel (for abroad), and other study-related expenses.
