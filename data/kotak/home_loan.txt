🏠 KOTAK HOME LOAN – FRESH HOME PURCHASE / CONSTRUCTION
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Loan for purchase of ready-to-move or under-construction residential property, or for construction/expansion/plot + house.

Interest Rate:  
• Floating: Starting @ 8.65% p.a. (indicative)  
• Salaried: 8.20%–8.50% p.a. for salaried/self-employed (approx)

Processing Fee:  
• 0.5% of loan amount for salaried  
• 1% for self-employed

Tenure:  
Up to 25 years (or as per bank / age limit)

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: Based on property cost and eligibility (no explicit minimum stated)  
• Maximum: Up to approx ₹40 crore (as per bank’s official data)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian / NRI  
• Minimum Age: 18 years  
• Maximum Age: 60 years (salaried) / 65 years (self-employed)

Documents Required:  
• Completed loan application with photographs  
• Proof of identity & address (PAN, Aadhaar, Driving License, Passport)  
• Salary slips (last 3–6 months) / bank statements (salaried)  
• Income tax returns / audited accounts (self-employed)  
• Property documents (Agreement for Sale, Sale Deed, Approved Plan, OC if ready)  

-----------------------------------------
👩‍💼 KOTAK HOME LOAN – HOME LOAN FOR WOMEN
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Special home loan scheme for women borrowers / co-borrowers, offering preferential rates and benefits.

Interest Rate:  
Starting from ~8.75% p.a. for women borrowers

Processing Fee:  
0.5% of loan amount for salaried women applicants (same as general salaried)

Tenure:  
Up to 25 years (subject to eligibility)

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: Based on eligibility and property cost  
• Maximum: Up to ₹40 crore (same as general home loan)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian woman or co-borrower pair with woman as primary applicant  
• Minimum Age: 18 years  
• Maximum Age: 60 years (salaried) or as per policy (self-employed)

Documents Required:  
• Standard home loan documents (as above)  
• Proof of income/employment of woman applicant or co-applicant  

-----------------------------------------
🏡 KOTAK HOME LOAN – BALANCE TRANSFER
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Facility to transfer existing home loan from another lender to Kotak to benefit from lower interest rates, longer tenure or lower EMI.

Interest Rate:  
Starting from ~8.00% p.a. (for salaried, balance transfer cases)

Processing Fee:  
• 0.5% of loan amount (salaried)  
• Up to 1% (self-employed)

Tenure:  
Up to 25 years (remaining or new tenure as per eligibility)

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: Outstanding amount of existing home loan  
• Maximum: As per original home loan eligibility (case-based)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian / NRI  
• Minimum Age: 18 years  
• Maximum Age: 60 years (salaried) / 65 years (self-employed)

Documents Required:  
• Balance transfer application form  
• Previous loan sanction letter and repayment statement  
• Property title and valuation report  
• Proof of identity, address, and income proof (if required)

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
-----------------------------------------
Q1: What is the processing fee for Kotak home loans?  
A: 0.5% of the loan amount for salaried and 1% for self-employed.

Q2: What is the minimum interest rate Kotak offers?  
A: Interest rates start from about 8.65% p.a. for eligible applicants.

Q3: What is the maximum tenure available?  
A: Tenure up to 25 years, subject to age and property eligibility.

Q4: Are there special offers for women borrowers?  
A: Yes, Kotak offers “Home Loan for Women” with rates starting ~8.75% p.a.

Q5: Are prepayment or foreclosure charges applicable?  
A: No, for floating rate home loans — foreclosure/prepayment is free.
