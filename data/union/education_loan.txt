🎓 UNION EDUCATION LOAN – INDIA & ABROAD
-----------------------------------
Description:  
General education loan to finance higher, technical, or professional education in India or abroad.

Interest Rate:  
~9.30% – 12.30% p.a. (indicative; varies by course/institution/profile)

Processing Fee:  
Nil in many cases (or as per bank schedule)

Tenure:  
Up to 15 years after course + moratorium period

Loan Amount Range:  
Unsecured up to ₹4 lakh for studies in India; higher limits with collateral for premium or overseas studies

Eligibility:  
• Applicant: Indian national (some variants may cover NRIs)  
• Course Type: Full-time recognised technical, management, professional, or doctoral programs in India or abroad

Documents Required:  
• KYC of applicant and co-applicant (parent/guardian)  
• Admission or offer letter from institution  
• Fee schedule, hostel, and living expense estimate  
• Academic records – marksheets and certificates  
• Income proof of co-applicant (salary/ITR/Form16)  
• Passport/visa and foreign admission proof (for abroad studies)

Features:  
• Covers tuition, hostel, books, equipment, and living costs  
• Moratorium = Course duration + 1 year grace before repayment  
• No collateral/guarantee for small loans (up to ₹4 lakh)  
• Repayment up to 15 years available

-----------------------------------
🏫 UNION SPECIAL EDUCATION LOAN SCHEME – PREMIER INSTITUTES
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Special education loan for students admitted to premier technical, management, or medical institutes in India or abroad.

Interest Rate:  
~7.70% – 9.50% p.a. (indicative)

Processing Fee:  
Nil or very nominal

Tenure:  
Up to 15 years (after moratorium)

Loan Amount Range:  
Up to ₹40 lakh (without collateral); higher with security

Eligibility:  
• Applicant: Students admitted to IITs, IIMs, BITS, or approved premier institutes via entrance exam  
• Course Type: Full-time UG/PG technical, management, or medical courses

Documents Required:  
• Admission letter confirming premier institute status  
• Standard KYC, income, and academic documents

Features:  
• Up to 100% cost coverage possible  
• No margin money in some schemes  
• No prepayment or processing charges for select institutions

-----------------------------------
🛠️ UNION SKILL DEVELOPMENT / VOCATIONAL EDUCATION LOAN
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Loan for vocational or skill development courses and short-term certification programs.

Interest Rate:  
~9% – 11.50% p.a. (varies by course)

Processing Fee:  
Nil or minimal for small-ticket loans

Tenure:  
Up to 2 years for small loans (~₹50,000); up to 7 years for larger ones

Loan Amount Range:  
From small-ticket (below ₹1 lakh) to higher as per program cost

Eligibility:  
• Applicant: Indian national enrolled in a recognised vocational/skill program (2 months to 3 years duration)

Documents Required:  
• Admission/certificate for course  
• Minimal income proof of co-applicant  
• KYC for applicant/co-applicant

Features:  
• Low documentation and fast approval  
• Suitable for short or modular training programs

-----------------------------------
💰 UNION EDUCATION FEE-BRIDGE / REIMBURSEMENT SUPPORT LOAN
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Bridge loan for students awaiting government fee reimbursement (e.g., SC/ST students).

Interest Rate:  
Aligned with standard education loan rates (as applicable)

Processing Fee:  
Nil or nominal

Tenure:  
Short-term; typically closed upon reimbursement or converted to a regular education loan

Loan Amount Range:  
Up to the fee amount awaiting reimbursement

Eligibility:  
• Applicant: SC/ST or students eligible for government reimbursement  
• Course Type: Recognised courses approved by government authority

Documents Required:  
• Proof of reimbursement eligibility  
• Admission/fee demand letter  
• KYC and co-applicant documents

Features:  
• Enables timely fee payment pending reimbursement  
• Converts to standard education loan if reimbursement delayed

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------
Q: What is the moratorium period for education loans?  
A: Course duration plus 1 year (or as per sanction).

Q: What is the maximum repayment tenure?  
A: Up to 15 years after moratorium.

Q: Is collateral required?  
A: Not for loans up to ₹4 lakh; above that, collateral/guarantee may be needed.

Q: Can the loan cover living expenses?  
A: Yes. Tuition, hostel, and living costs are covered.

Q: Are there processing fees?  
A: Many schemes are nil or charge minimal fees.

Q: Can I apply online?  
A: Yes – visit https://educationloan.unionbankofindia.co.in/

Q: What are the rates for studying abroad?  
A: Around 8–10% p.a. for premier institution programs.

Q: Can I prepay early?  
A: Yes. No penalty in most schemes.

Q: What if reimbursement is delayed?  
A: Fee-bridge loans convert to standard education loans.

Q: Are skill/vocational courses eligible?  
A: Yes, under the Skill Development Loan scheme.

Q: What margin is required?  
A: No margin up to ₹4 lakh; above that, standard margins apply.

Q: Can NRIs apply?  
A: Generally limited to Indian nationals; NRI cases subject to special terms.

Q: When is the loan disbursed?  
A: After document verification; faster for online/small-ticket loans.

Q: Is there an interest rebate during study?  
A: Some schemes offer rebates if interest is serviced during moratorium.
