🏠 UNION HOME LOAN
-----------------------------------
Description:  
For purchase or construction of house/flat, extension or repair of existing house, or purchase of plot + construction.  
Available to Resident Indians and NRIs.

Interest Rate:  
Starting from 8.35% p.a.

Processing Fee:  
Up to 0.50% of the loan amount (Min ₹5,000, Max ₹15,000) + GST

Tenure:  
Up to 30 years

Loan Amount Range:  
₹2 lakh – ₹10 crore

Eligibility:  
• Applicant Type: Salaried or self-employed individuals  
• Minimum Age: 21 years  
• Maximum Age: 70 years at loan maturity  
• Co-applicant: Allowed for family members

Features:  
• Flexible repayment up to 30 years  
• Top-up facility available  
• Attractive interest rates linked to credit score  
• Concession for women borrowers

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------
Q: How can I apply for this loan?  
A: You can apply online through the Union Bank of India website or visit the nearest branch with required documents.

Q: What is the processing time for the loan?  
A: Loan approval typically takes 3 to 7 working days depending on documentation and eligibility.

Q: Can I prepay or foreclose the loan?  
A: Yes, prepayment or foreclosure is allowed anytime. No prepayment penalty applies on floating rate loans.

Q: How is EMI calculated?  
A: EMI is based on loan amount, interest rate, and tenure using the standard reducing balance method.

Q: Are women borrowers eligible for interest concessions?  
A: Yes, women borrowers enjoy special interest rate concessions under select Union Bank schemes.
