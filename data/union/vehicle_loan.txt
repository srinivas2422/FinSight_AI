🚗 UNION MILES – NEW CAR LOAN
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Finance for purchase of new passenger cars (for personal and business use) with competitive interest rates and flexible tenures.

Interest Rate:  
Starting from 8.70% p.a. (subject to applicant profile and dealer tie-ups)

Processing Fee:  
Up to 1% of loan amount + GST (subject to minimum)

Tenure:  
Up to 7 years (84 months)

Loan Amount Range:  
₹1 lakh – ₹50 lakh (up to a percentage of on-road price)

Eligibility:  
• Applicant Type: Salaried or self-employed individuals/professionals  
• Age Limit: Minimum 21 years; maximum at maturity typically 70 years  
• Income Criteria: Minimum net monthly income as per branch norms; good credit record preferred

Documents Required:  
General Documents:  
• KYC documents (PAN, Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport)  
• Completed application form with photographs  
• Proof of income: Salary slips / ITR / Bank statements  

Vehicle Documents:  
• Proforma invoice / quotation from dealer (for new cars)  
• Insurance quotation / vehicle details for registration

Features:  
• Finance up to 85% of on-road price for new cars  
• No foreclosure charges after initial lock-in period (scheme-dependent)  
• EMI holiday options available in select schemes  
• Top-up and balance transfer options available

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------
Q: What documents are required to apply for a vehicle loan?  
A: KYC documents, proof of income (salary slips or ITR), vehicle proforma invoice/quotation, and photographs are typically required.

Q: Can I finance a used car?  
A: Yes. Union Bank offers used car loans depending on vehicle age and condition; interest rates are usually higher.

Q: What is the maximum tenure for vehicle loans?  
A: Up to 7 years for passenger cars; longer tenors available for select EV or commercial loans.

Q: Is prepayment allowed for vehicle loans?  
A: Yes, prepayment is allowed after an initial period; check specific scheme for charges.

Q: How much of the on-road price can be financed?  
A: Up to 85% of on-road price for new cars; lower for used/commercial vehicles.

Q: Are there special schemes for electric vehicles?  
A: Yes, Union Green Vehicle Loan offers concessional rates and extended tenure for EVs.

Q: Can businesses or fleet operators apply?  
A: Yes, commercial and fleet vehicle loans are available for registered entities.

Q: Is insurance mandatory?  
A: Yes, valid motor insurance (minimum third-party) is required before disbursal.

Q: How long does loan disbursal take?  
A: Typically 2–7 working days after complete documentation; may vary for used vehicles.

Q: Can I transfer an existing vehicle loan to Union Bank?  
A: Yes, balance transfer is available subject to eligibility and processing charges.

Q: Are top-up loans available?  
A: In some cases, subject to repayment track record and scheme type.

Q: How is interest calculated?  
A: Interest is applied on a monthly reducing balance basis.
