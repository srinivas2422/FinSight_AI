🏅 UNION SWARNA SHAKTI (RETAIL GOLD LOAN)
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Quick collateralised loan against gold jewellery and coins for personal and household needs. Minimal documentation and same-day disbursal.

Interest Rate:  
Starting from 9.95% p.a. (monthly or annual interest options available)

Processing Fee:  
Nominal processing/handling charges (₹100 – ₹500) + GST; exact charge at branch discretion

Tenure:  
Up to 12 months (renewable subject to pledge renewal)

Loan Amount Range:  
₹5,000 – ₹20 lakh (subject to purity, weight and valuation)

Eligibility:  
• Applicant Type: Resident individuals (heads of household or owners of gold)  
• Age Limit: 18 – 70 years  
• Collateral: Gold ornaments / coins of acceptable purity (typically 22K/18K depending on scheme)

Documents Required:  
• KYC documents (Aadhaar / PAN / Voter ID / Passport / Driving Licence)  
• Loan application form with photographs  
• Details of gold pledged (weight, estimated purity)  
• Any previous pledge receipts (if renewal)  
• Proof of address (if required)  
• Bank account details for repayment

Features:  
• High LTV up to 75% of assessed gold value (as per RBI norms)  
• Immediate disbursal upon valuation and pledge  
• Flexible repayment – interest-only, EMI, or bullet repayment  
• Secure custody and insurance of pledged gold

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------
Q: What types of gold are accepted?  
A: Hallmarked jewellery and approved coins; purity (22K/18K) assessed by bank valuer.

Q: What is the typical Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio?  
A: Up to 75% of gold’s assessed value, per RBI and bank policy.

Q: How quickly can I get funds after pledging gold?  
A: Disbursal is generally same-day post valuation and verification.

Q: Is there insurance for stored gold?  
A: Yes, gold pledged is kept under bank insurance coverage; applicable charges may be recovered.

Q: Can I renew my gold loan on maturity?  
A: Yes, subject to revaluation and branch approval.

Q: Are there concessional rates for farmers?  
A: Yes, under Union Krishi Kamdhenu and other priority sector categories.

Q: What happens if I default on repayment?  
A: Bank issues notices; gold may be auctioned after due process; penalty and recovery rules apply.

Q: Can antique jewellery be pledged?  
A: Accepted only if purity and value can be verified; heavily studded items may be restricted.

Q: Do I need to provide income proof?  
A: Not required for small retail loans; required for MSME or high-value loans.

Q: Can NRIs pledge gold in India?  
A: Yes, with valid documentation and proof of ownership.

Q: Are there prepayment charges?  
A: Generally none for short-term gold loans; check individual branch policy.

Q: Can co-owners apply jointly?  
A: Yes, joint applications permitted subject to documentation and ownership proof.

Q: How is gold valued?  
A: Based on daily gold rates and purity after bank’s authorized testing.

Q: What are loan limits per borrower?  
A: Limits vary by collateral, valuation, and branch authority levels.

Q: What happens after loan closure?  
A: Borrower receives pledged gold and closure receipt upon full repayment.
