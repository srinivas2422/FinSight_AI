💼 UNION PERSONAL LOAN – NON SALARIED (UNSECURED)
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Unsecured personal loan for non-salaried individuals to meet personal needs like wedding, education, travel, medical or debt consolidation.

Interest Rate:  
Starting from 8.25% p.a. (depends on profile and salary tie-ups)

Processing Fee:  
1% of loan amount (Min ₹500) + GST (subject to salary account concessions)

Tenure:  
Up to 5 years (may extend to 7 years for salaried professionals)

Loan Amount Range:  
₹50,000 – ₹15 lakh (depending on salary and employer tie-ups)

Eligibility:  
• Applicant Type: Non-salaried employees with at least 1–2 years of continuous service  
• Income Criteria: Applicant must maintain savings/current account with Union Bank with avg. quarterly balance ≥ ₹25,000 for preceding 4 quarters; account must show satisfactory conduct (no cheque returns, etc.)  
• Age Limit: 25 – 60 years at loan maturity

Documents Required:  
• KYC: PAN, Aadhaar, photos, application form  
• Proof of Address: Telephone bill, electricity bill, rent agreement, ration card, or other valid letter  
• Bank Statement: Last 6 months’ account statement

Features:  
• Unsecured finance with quick disbursal  
• No collateral required  
• Flexible EMIs and part prepayment options  
• Preferential pricing for salary account holders

👨‍⚕️ UNION PROFESSIONAL PERSONAL LOAN SCHEME
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Personal loan for self-employed professionals (doctors, CAs, architects, lawyers) for professional or personal expenses.

Interest Rate:  
Starting from 11.00% p.a. (profile dependent)

Processing Fee:  
0.75% – 1% + GST (depending on loan size)

Tenure:  
Up to 7 years

Loan Amount Range:  
₹1 lakh – ₹20 lakh

Eligibility:  
• Applicant Type: Practicing professionals with minimum 2 years’ experience  
• Income Proof: ITR, audited financials, establishment proof  
• Age Limit: 21 – 65 years at loan maturity

Documents Required:  
• Professional Proof: Degree, registration, practice certificate, client references  
• Income Proof: ITR for last 2 years, bank statements

Features:  
• Higher loan limits for established professionals  
• Flexible repayment terms  
• Competitive interest rates for qualified borrowers

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------
Q: Who can apply for a personal loan?  
A: Salaried, self-employed, and business owners who meet income and credit norms.

Q: What is the typical interest rate?  
A: Usually ranges from 10.20% – 12.50% p.a. depending on scheme and credit score.

Q: How long does disbursal take?  
A: Generally 2–7 working days for salaried; longer for self-employed due to verification.

Q: Can I prepay my loan?  
A: Yes, prepayment is allowed; charges depend on product type and tenure.

Q: Do I need collateral?  
A: No. Most personal loans are unsecured.

Q: Can I transfer my existing personal loan to Union Bank?  
A: Balance transfer possible subject to policy and eligibility.

Q: What documents are needed?  
A: KYC (PAN, Aadhaar), income proof (salary slips/ITR), bank statements, etc.

Q: Is there a minimum credit score?  
A: A good credit history is required; higher scores improve eligibility.

Q: Are there special schemes for women/professionals?  
A: Yes, Women Professional Loan and Professional Loan offer tailored benefits.

Q: What are the fees and charges?  
A: Processing fee, GST, late payment, and prepayment charges per schedule.

Q: How is EMI calculated?  
A: On monthly reducing balance method based on amount, tenure, and rate.

Q: Is there a cooling-off period?  
A: Yes, as per branch sanction policy; discuss before disbursal.

Q: Can I get a top-up?  
A: Possible for eligible borrowers with good repayment track record.

Q: What happens on default?  
A: Penal interest, CIBIL reporting, and recovery procedures apply.
