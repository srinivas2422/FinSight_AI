🏠 HOME LOAN
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Apply online for a Home Loan with instant provisional sanction, minimal documentation, and long tenure. Financing for purchase, construction, or renovation of residential property.

Interest Rate:  
• Salaried –  
  - Up to ₹35 lakh: 8.75% – 9.40%  
  - ₹35–75 lakh: 8.75% – 9.55%  
  - Above ₹75 lakh: 8.75% – 9.65%  
• Self-Employed –  
  - Up to ₹35 lakh: 8.75% – 9.55%  
  - ₹35–75 lakh: 8.75% – 9.70%  
  - Above ₹75 lakh: 8.75% – 9.80%  

Instant Home Loan (Digital): 7.70% p.a. (subject to credit/bureau score; digital journey)  
Fixed Rate Options: 24 months fixed: 8.85% p.a.; 37 months fixed: 8.65%–8.80% p.a.; full-term fixed: 10.90%–11.20% p.a.

Processing Fee:  
0.50% of the loan amount + applicable taxes

Tenure:  
Up to 30 years

Loan Amount Range:  
Up to ₹5 crore (subject to eligibility and property value)

Eligibility:  
• Applicant Type: Salaried / Self-Employed  
• Age:  
  - Salaried: 20–65 years (at loan maturity)  
  - Self-Employed: 21–70 years (at loan maturity)  
• Credit Score: Ideally 750+  
• Other:  
  - Income stability and repayment capacity assessed by bank  
  - Property legal and technical verification as per policy  

Documents Required:  
• General: Identity Proof, Address Proof, Income Proof  
• Property: Title / Approval / Agreement Documents  

Features:  
• Instant provisional sanction (online)  
• Repo-linked transparent rates  
• Nil foreclosure charges on floating-rate loans  
• Online EMI/Eligibility/Affordability calculators

Repo-linked rates applicable to NRI profile (refer to standard grid).
