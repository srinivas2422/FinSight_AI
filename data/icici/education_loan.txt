🎓 EDUCATION LOAN
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Standard Education Loan for higher studies in India and abroad covering tuition fees and eligible living/travel/other academic expenses.

Interest Rate:  
- Reference: ICICI Bank Education Loan Interest Rates (linked to I-EBLR)  
- Engineering / Management (Select Domestic Institutes):  
  • Secured: 9.00% p.a.  
  • Unsecured: 9.75% p.a.  
- Others (Loan amount ≥ ₹15 Lakh):  
  • Secured: 9.25% p.a.  
  • Unsecured: 10.25% p.a.  
- Medical (Loan ≥ ₹20 Lakh):  
  • Secured: 9.25% p.a.  
  • Unsecured: 10.25% p.a.  
- iSMART Range (Q2 FY25):  
  • Min: 10.25% p.a.  
  • Max: 14.00% p.a.  
  • Mean: 11.27% p.a.  

Processing Fee:  
As per ICICI Bank "Education Loan: Fees & Service Charges" schedule (refer official page).

Tenure:  
Up to 15 years (course period + repayment)

Loan Amount Range:  
Up to ₹3 crore (for studies abroad) / up to ₹1 crore (for studies in India)

Eligibility:  
• Nationality: Indian  
• Age: 16–35 years  
• Academic Record: Proven good academic performance  
• Qualification: Completed 10+2 or Diploma  
• Course: Graduate / Postgraduate / PG Diploma in recognised & accredited institutes (India or abroad)

Documents Required:  
• KYC: PAN, Identity & Address proof  
• Academics & Admission: Offer/Admission letter, Fee structure/estimate  
• Co-applicant/Income/Collateral: Income proofs, Collateral documents (if secured)

Features:  
• Covers tuition + living/travel/academic expenses  
• Moratorium during course + grace period  
• Section 80E tax benefit on interest (as applicable)

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------------
Q: What is the maximum loan amount under ICICI Education Loans?  
A: Up to ₹3 crore (secured) for studies abroad and ₹1 crore (unsecured) for select courses.

Q: Is there a collateral-free loan option?  
A: Yes — Instant Education Loan and iSmart variants offer collateral-free options up to ₹1 crore.

Q: What is the repayment period?  
A: Up to 15 years including moratorium (course duration + grace).

Q: Are loans available for studying abroad?  
A: Yes — ICICI offers specific products for foreign universities and overseas programs.

Q: Can I get a pre-admission sanction?  
A: Yes, for eligible applicants under the foreign education loan.

Q: What tax benefits are available?  
A: Interest repayment qualifies for deduction under Section 80E.

Q: What documents are needed?  
A: KYC, admission letter, fee structure, academic proof, and co-applicant income/collateral papers.

Q: Are digital loans available?  
A: Yes — iSmart and Instant variants provide 100% online application and sanction.

Q: Who can be a co-applicant?  
A: Parent, guardian, or spouse (as per eligibility).

Q: Are there any prepayment charges?  
A: Typically nil on education loan prepayments.
