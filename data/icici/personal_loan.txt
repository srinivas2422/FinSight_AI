💰 PERSONAL LOAN
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Unsecured personal loan for salaried and self-employed customers with full end-use flexibility and a 100% digital application process.

Interest Rate:  
Between 10.60% and 16.50% p.a. (as per ICICI Bank disclosure for Apr–Jun 2025).

Processing Fee:  
Up to 2% of the loan amount + applicable taxes.

Tenure:  
12 to 72 months.

Loan Amount Range:  
₹50,000 – ₹50,00,000.

Interest Rate / Eligibility Factors:  
• Credit profile, score, and repayment history.  
• Customer segment and geographic location.  
• Income level and employer category (for salaried).  
• Loan amount and chosen tenure.

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian  
• Minimum Age: 21 years (at application)  
• Maximum Age: As per bank policy at loan maturity  
• Income: As per bank policy (salaried/self-employed)

Documents Required:  
• KYC: PAN, Identity proof, Address proof  
• Salaried: Last 3 salary slips, 6 months bank statements, Form 16/ITR  
• Self-Employed: ITR (last 2–3 years), Bank statements (6 months), Business proof

Features:  
• Collateral-free; quick disbursal.  
• Online eligibility and EMI calculator.  
• Prepayment/foreclosure as per bank policy.

Starting at 10.85% p.a. (as per ICICI Bank Balance Transfer page).

Processing Fee:  
Nominal (as per current charges).

Tenure:  
Typically within 12–72 months (depending on eligibility and remaining term).

Loan Amount Range:  
As per eligibility, based on existing outstanding and credit profile.

Interest Rate / Eligibility Factors:

-----------------------------------------
📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------------
Q: What is the maximum personal loan amount available?  
A: Up to ₹50 lakh, depending on eligibility and income profile.

Q: Is collateral required for ICICI Personal Loan?  
A: No, all personal loans are unsecured.

Q: Can I get an instant personal loan?  
A: Yes — pre-qualified customers can apply via iMobile or Internet Banking.

Q: What is the typical tenure?  
A: 12 to 72 months, depending on scheme and eligibility.

Q: Can I transfer my loan from another bank?  
A: Yes, through the Personal Loan Balance Transfer scheme.

Q: When can I get a top-up loan?  
A: Generally after paying at least 6 EMIs with good repayment track.

Q: Are there foreclosure charges?  
A: None after 12 EMIs for instant variant; otherwise as per policy.

Q: What factors affect interest rate?  
A: Credit score, income, segment, and repayment behaviour.

Q: Is the process fully digital?  
A: Yes, end-to-end digital journey for eligible customers.
