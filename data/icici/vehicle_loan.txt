🚗 NEW CAR LOAN
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Financing for a brand-new car with up to 100% on-road funding and flexible tenure.

Interest Rate (Q1 FY25 Reference):  
• Minimum: 8.50% p.a.  
• Maximum: 11.25% p.a.  
• Mean: 9.47% p.a.  
(As per ICICI Bank Car Loan – Interest Rate Range, Quarter Ended Jun 2025)

Processing Fee:  
As per ICICI Bank Car Loan “Service Charges” schedule (processing/login fee as applicable)

Tenure:  
Up to 84 months

Loan Amount Range:  
As per eligibility (up to 100% on-road funding possible)

Eligibility:  
• Applicant: Indian salaried or self-employed individual meeting bank’s credit/income norms  
• Key Factors:  
  - CIBIL/credit score  
  - Income and obligations  
  - Car model/segment  
  - Existing relationship with the bank  

Documents Required:  
• KYC: PAN, Identity & Address proof  
• Income: Latest salary slip/Form 16 (salaried), ITR/Financials (self-employed)  
• Vehicle: Dealer proforma invoice or quotation  

Features:  
• Up to 100% on-road funding  
• Digital application with quick approval  
• Flexible tenure up to 84 months

🚘 REFINANCE CAR LOAN (LOAN AGAINST CAR / BALANCE TRANSFER-REFINANCE)
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Refinance your existing car (with nil or closed loan) or transfer an ongoing car loan to ICICI Bank for better terms or fresh funds.

Interest Rate:  
As per prevailing ICICI Bank car loan rates for refinance (within published new/used car ranges)

Processing Fee:  
As per ICICI Bank Car Loan “Service Charges” schedule

Tenure:  
Up to 84 months (subject to vehicle age and bank policy)

Loan Amount Range:  
Up to 140% of the car’s valuation (as per policy and eligibility)

Eligibility:  
• Applicant: Salaried or self-employed meeting bank’s criteria  
• Vehicle/Loan:  
  - Car not hypothecated (or closed loan) for refinance  
  - For balance transfer: Good repayment track record  

Documents Required:  
• KYC: PAN, Identity & Address proof  
• Existing Loan (if BT): Loan statement, Repayment record, NOC from previous lender (if applicable)  
• Vehicle: RC book, Insurance, Pollution Certificate  

Features:  
• Refinance up to 140% of valuation (per policy)  
• Fast processing and simple documentation  
• Option to lower EMI or extend tenure
