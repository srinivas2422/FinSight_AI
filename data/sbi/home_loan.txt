💡 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
-----------------------------------
1️⃣ What is Pre-EMI?  
➡️ Pay only interest on disbursed amount till construction completes; EMIs start post-possession.  

2️⃣ What is covered under Home Insurance?  
➡️ House structure + contents (furniture, electronics, jewelry).  

3️⃣ Can Home Loan be Pre-approved?  
➡️ Yes, based on income and repayment capacity.  

4️⃣ What is EBLR?  
➡️ External Benchmark Lending Rate — linked to RBI Repo Rate since Oct 2019.  

5️⃣ Do women get concessional rates?  
➡️ Yes, 0.05% p.a. interest concession if woman is main applicant/co-applicant.  

6️⃣ What is the maximum tenure?  
➡️ Up to 30 years or age 70, whichever earlier.  

7️⃣ Can I sell property while loan is active?  
➡️ Yes, after bank’s consent; documents released after full repayment.  

8️⃣ What is a down payment?  
➡️ 15–25% of property cost paid upfront; bank finances the remaining.  

9️⃣ How is interest calculated?  
➡️ On daily reducing balance basis — charged only on outstanding principal.  

10️⃣ Where can I apply?  
➡️ Via website, YONO App, or nearest SBI Branch/Home Loan Centre.

📜 TERMS AND CONDITIONS
-----------------------------------
Purpose:  
Purchase/Construction/Extension/Repairs/Renovation/Furnishing of residential house/flat/plot  

Interest Rate:  
- Floating Rate linked to EBLR (Repo Rate-based)  
- Reset on RBI Repo change  
- EMI may be adjusted accordingly  

Penal Charges:  
- 2.40% p.a. up to 60 days irregularity  
- 5% p.a. beyond 60 days  
- ₹250 + GST per bounced cheque/NACH  
- 2% p.a. penalty for delay in mortgage creation  

Repayment:  
- Equated Monthly Instalments (EMI)  
- Auto-debit/NACH with 120% limit for rate fluctuation  
- Start date as per sanction letter  

Pre-closure:  
- No penalty on floating-rate loans  

Security:  
- Mortgage of property  
- Collateral/guarantee as required  
- Interim security till mortgage creation  

Insurance:  
- Property must be insured against fire, flood, earthquake, etc., in joint names  

Inspection:  
- Bank reserves right to inspect property  

Charges & Fees:  
- Processing Fee: 0.35% + GST (Min ₹2,000, Max ₹10,000)  
- Legal & Valuation: As per actuals  
- CERSAI: ₹100 + GST  
- Safe Custody: ₹1000 + GST/quarter (if deeds not collected)  
- Conversion/Switch Fee: ₹1000–₹5000 + GST  
- EMI Missed: ₹250 + GST  

Disbursement:  
- After security execution  
- Staged release for construction  
- Direct credit to builder/seller
