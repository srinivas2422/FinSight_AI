🏫 Student Loan Scheme
-----------------------------------
Description:  
A term loan for Indian nationals for higher education in India or abroad where admission has been secured. Covers undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional/technical courses.

Interest Rate:  
- Without Collateral: 10.15% p.a. (up to ₹7.50 lakh)  
- With Collateral: 9.15% p.a. (above ₹7.50 lakh)  

Processing Fee:  
- Up to ₹20 lakh: Nil  
- Above ₹20 lakh: ₹10,000 + taxes  

Tenure:  
Up to 15 years after commencement of repayment (moratorium = course period + 12 months)

Loan Amount Range:  
- In India: Up to ₹75 lakh for MBBS/MD/MS; up to ₹50 lakh for other courses (in some cases up to ₹1 crore)  
- Abroad: Up to ₹7.50 lakh in this scheme (higher for other schemes)

Eligibility:  
- Resident Type: Resident Indian  
- Minimum Age: 18 years  
- Maximum Age: 70 years (verify with bank)

Documents Required:  
- Completed loan application form with 3 passport-size photographs  
- Mark sheets of 10th, 12th, and previous qualification certificates  
- Proof of admission / offer letter from educational institution  
- Schedule of course expenses, proof of scholarship/assistantship (if any)  
- Parent/guardian as co-borrower (for loans up to ₹7.50 lakh) or collateral plus co-borrower (for above ₹7.50 lakh)

🎓 Scholar Loan Scheme
-----------------------------------
Description:  
For students admitted to premier institutions (e.g., IITs, IIMs, NITs, etc.) with preferential terms including 100% financing and no processing fee.

Interest Rate: 7.15% – 7.90% p.a.  
Processing Fee: Nil  
Tenure: Up to 15 years after course period + 12 months repayment holiday  
Loan Amount Range: Varies by institution category (up to ₹40 lakh or more)

Eligibility:  
- Resident Type: Indian National / OCI / PIO / NRI (in some cases)  
- Minimum Age: 18 years  
- Maximum Age: Not specified  

Documents Required:  
- Completed application form with photographs  
- Admission letter from selected institution, proof of expenses (fees, equipment, travel, etc.)  
- Parent/guardian as co-borrower or collateral as required by bank  


-----------------------------------
🌍 Global Ed-Vantage Scheme
-----------------------------------
Description:  
For students pursuing higher studies abroad; loans available with collateral for larger amounts.

Interest Rate:  
- With Collateral: 9.15% p.a. (above ₹7.50 lakh up to ₹3.00 crore)  
- Other: Varies depending on collateral & institution category  

Processing Fee: ₹10,000 per application  
Tenure: Up to 15 years after course completion; repayment may start 6 months after completion.  
Loan Amount Range: Above ₹7.50 lakh up to ₹3.00 crore  

Eligibility:  
- Resident Type: Indian National  
- Minimum Age: 18 years  
- Maximum Age: Not specified  

Documents Required:  
- Schedule of expenses for the course abroad  
- Mark sheets and admission proof  
- Collateral/third-party guarantee documents if required

🛡️ Shaurya Education Loan
-----------------------------------
Description:  
For wards/spouse of Defence, Indian Coast Guard & Central Armed Police personnel.

Interest Rate:  
- Without Collateral: 9.15% p.a. (above ₹7.50 lakh up to ₹40 lakh)  
- With Collateral: 9.15% p.a. (above ₹7.50 lakh up to ₹1.50 crore)  

Processing Fee: Not clearly listed (likely similar to main scheme)  
Tenure: Up to 15 years after course period (moratorium similar to other schemes)  

Loan Amount Range:  
- In India: Up to ₹40 lakh  
- Abroad: Up to ₹1.50 crore  

Eligibility:  
- Resident Type: Indian National (ward/spouse of Defence/Coast Guard/CAPF personnel)  
- Minimum Age: 18 years  
- Maximum Age: Not specified  

Documents Required:  
- Standard education loan documents  
- Defence eligibility proof (ward/spouse status)  
- Collateral documents (if required)

❓ FAQs
-----------------------------------
1. **What is the interest rate for SBI Education Loans?**  
   The rate starts at ~7.15% p.a. (Scholar Loan) and goes up to 10.15% p.a. (Student Loan Scheme).

2. **Is processing fee charged for education loans?**  
   No processing fee for loans up to ₹20 lakh in India; ₹10,000 + taxes for larger or abroad-study loans.

3. **Is collateral required?**  
   Not required up to ₹7.50 lakh; required above ₹7.50 lakh.

4. **When does repayment start?**  
   After course completion + 12 months (or 6 months for abroad cases); tenure up to 15 years.

5. **Is there a concession for female students?**  
   Yes — 0.50% p.a. interest concession.

6. **Does the loan cover living/travel expenses?**  
   Yes, it includes tuition, hostel, library, lab, books, equipment, and travel expenses.
