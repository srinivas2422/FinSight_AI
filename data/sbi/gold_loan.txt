🏦 SBI Personal Gold Loan
-----------------------------------
Description:  
A general-purpose loan secured against gold ornaments and bank-minted gold coins (maximum 50 grams).  
Suitable for sudden financial needs such as medical, education, travel, or margin for home/education loans.

Interest Rate:  
- ~8.75% p.a. for 3-month bullet repayment  
- ~9.05% p.a. for 12-month bullet repayment  
- ~10.00% p.a. for EMI-based loans

Processing Fee:  
- 3-Month Bullet: Rs. 200 + GST  
- 6-Month Bullet: Rs. 300 + GST  
- 12-Month Bullet: 0.50% of loan amount (min Rs. 500, max Rs. 10,000) + GST  
- EMI-Based: 0.50% of loan amount (min Rs. 500) + GST

Tenure:  
3 months / 6 months / 12 months (bullet repayment)  
or up to 36 months (EMI-based)

Loan Amount Range:  
- Minimum: ₹20,000 (approx)  
- Maximum: Up to ₹50 lakh (approx)

Eligibility:  
- Resident Type: Indian National (NRIs eligible with local address)  
- Minimum Age: 18 years and above  
- Maximum Age: Not clearly specified (subject to bank policy)

Documents Required:  
- Two passport-size photographs  
- Proof of identity and address (Officially Valid Documents)

💡 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
-----------------------------------
Q1. What is the interest rate for SBI Gold Loans?  
A1. Starts from ~8.75% p.a. for short-term bullet schemes and about 10.00% p.a. for EMI-based loans.

Q2. What processing fee is charged on SBI Gold Loans?  
A2.  
- 3-month bullet: Rs. 200 + GST  
- 6-month bullet: Rs. 300 + GST  
- 12-month bullet: 0.50% of loan amount (min Rs. 500, max Rs. 10,000) + GST  
- EMI-based: 0.50% of loan amount (min Rs. 500) + GST

Q3. What is the maximum loan amount I can avail under SBI Gold Loan?  
A3. Up to approximately ₹50 lakh, depending on gold value, purity, and bank margin, against ornaments or specially minted gold coins (max 50g).

Q4. Are there any pre-payment or foreclosure charges on SBI Gold Loan?  
A4. No prepayment or foreclosure charges are applicable.

Q5. Who is eligible for SBI Gold Loan?  
A5. Any individual aged 18 years and above with a steady income and gold to pledge. NRIs are eligible with a local address.
