🏦 SBI PERSONAL LOAN (GENERAL SALARIED EMPLOYEES)
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Personal loan to salaried employees having salary package account with SBI for any general purpose such as wedding, vacation, medical, education, etc.

Interest Rate:  
From ~10.05% p.a to ~15.05% p.a depending on tenure, amount and profile.

Processing Fee:  
Up to 1.50% of the loan amount (minimum ₹1,000 & maximum ₹15,000) + GST.

Tenure:  
Minimum 6 months – Maximum up to 84 months (7 years) or till retirement/contract end as applicable.

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: ₹1 lakh (approx)  
• Maximum: Up to ₹35 lakh (or 24× net monthly income, whichever is lower)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Indian National  
• Minimum Age: 21 years  
• Maximum Age: 60 years (for salaried employees)

Documents Required:  
• Completed application form with 2-3 passport size photographs  
• PAN card  
• Officially Valid Document (OVD) for identity  
• Last 6 months salary slips  
• Last 6 months salary account statement where salary credited

🏦 SBI REAL-TIME XPRESS CREDIT (RTXC)
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Fully-digital personal loan for salary-package account customers of SBI offering instant sanction and disbursement.

Interest Rate:  
Profile & scheme based; approx 11%-14% p.a.

Processing Fee:  
Up to 1.50% of loan amount (minimum ₹1,000 & maximum ₹15,000) + GST.

Tenure:  
6 months – 72 months (6 years)

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: ₹1 lakh  
• Maximum: Up to ₹35 lakh / 24× NMI (salary), whichever is lower

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Indian National (salary account customer of SBI)  
• Minimum Age: Not explicitly stated (salary account holder, NMI > ₹25,000)  
• Maximum Age: Not clearly specified  

Documents Required:  
• Digital documentation via YONO / salary account credentials  
• Standard KYC / salary proof as per bank’s salary-package norms

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------
Q: What is the interest rate for SBI personal loans?  
A: Starts from ~10.05% p.a up to ~15.05% p.a depending on profile, loan amount, and tenure.

Q: What is the processing fee for personal loans at SBI?  
A: Up to 1.50% of loan amount (minimum ₹1,000, maximum ₹15,000) + GST.

Q: Is security or guarantor required?  
A: No, SBI personal loans are unsecured.

Q: What is the maximum tenure?  
A: Up to 84 months (7 years) or till retirement/contract end.

Q: Are prepayments allowed?  
A: Yes, prepayment/part-closure is allowed with 3% charge (for Xpress Credit group), unless repaid from new SBI loan.

Q: How much can I borrow?  
A: Up to ₹35 lakh, subject to 24× NMI and internal eligibility criteria.

📄 TERMS & CONDITIONS
-----------------------------------
Purpose:  
Unsecured Personal Loan (Xpress Credit / Xpress Power / Pension Loans) for any general purpose including consumption, tourism, marriage, medical, education, or foreign travel — except speculative activities.

Eligibility:  
Salaried employees and pensioners with regular income; also available via YONO or Internet Banking for pre-approved customers.

Rate of Interest:  
Fixed for the loan tenure; charged on daily reducing balance with monthly rests.

Change in Rate:  
Fixed rate — no impact of MCLR changes; no separate notice required.

Mode of Repayment:  
Check-off facility or SI/NACH/ECS/ACH from salary/pension account.  
Can also repay via cheque or cash.

Margin:  
Nil (for Xpress Credit / Power / Pension loans).

Processing Fee:  
• Xpress Credit & Power: 1% + applicable tax  
• Pension Loan: 0.50% + tax (min ₹250)  
Concessions may apply under specific tie-ups or promotions.

Repayment:  
EMI-based as per sanction letter; repayment starts on date specified.

Pre-closure Charges:  
3% for Xpress Credit/Power/Pension Loans. Nil if repaid from fresh SBI loan.

Other Fees:  
Legal opinion, valuation, stamp duty, registration, etc., as per actuals.

Security:  
No collateral required.

Penal Interest:  
2% per month (over and above applicable interest) on overdue amount if irregularity >1 month.

Timeline for Disposal:  
2–7 working days after complete document submission.
