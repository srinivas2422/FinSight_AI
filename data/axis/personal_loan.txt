💰 PERSONAL LOAN
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Unsecured personal loan for multiple needs (wedding, travel, education, medical, home renovation, etc.) with a fully digital process and quick disbursal.

Interest Rate:  
Fixed; 9.99% p.a. – 22% p.a. (linked to MCLR spread; profile and tenure dependent)

Processing Fee:  
Up to 2% of loan amount + applicable GST

Tenure:  
12 to 84 months

Loan Amount Range:  
₹50,000 – ₹40,00,000 (subject to eligibility)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian  
• Minimum Age: 21 years at application  
• Maximum Age: 60 years at loan maturity  
• Minimum Monthly Income:  
  - Existing Axis Bank Customer: ₹15,000  
  - New-to-Axis Customer: ₹25,000  
• Other:  
  - Salaried or self-employed (as per policy)  
  - Credit profile as per bank norms  

Documents Required:  
• KYC:  
  - PAN  
  - Identity Proof (Passport/Driving Licence/Voter ID/Aadhaar)  
  - Address Proof (Passport/Driving Licence/Aadhaar/Utility Bill)  
• Income Proof:  
  - Latest 2 months’ salary slips & bank statements (salaried)  
  - ITR/financials & bank statements (self-employed)  
• Other:  
  - Completed application form with photo  
  - Self-attestation on all documents  

Features:  
• End-to-end digital application with Video KYC  
• Instant eligibility check and quick disbursal  
• No collateral or guarantor required  
• EMI calculators & eligibility tools available online
