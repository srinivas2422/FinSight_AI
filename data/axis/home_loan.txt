🏠 AXIS BANK HOME LOAN (STANDARD)
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Home loan for purchase, construction, extension, or repair of residential property for Resident Indians and NRIs.

Interest Rate:  
• Floating: 8.75% p.a. onwards  
• Typical Range: 8.75% – 9.15% p.a. (for CIBIL 751+)  
• Fixed Rate: 14.00% p.a.  

Processing Fee:  
Up to 1% of loan amount + GST (minimum ₹10,000)

Tenure:  
Up to 30 years or as per borrower’s age & policy.

Loan Amount Range:  
Up to ₹5 crore (subject to property value, income, and eligibility)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian / NRI  
• Minimum Age: 21 years at loan commencement  
• Maximum Age: 60 years (salaried) / 65 years (self-employed)  
• Income & Credit: Salaried/self-employed with stable income and good credit history  
• Property and co-applicant must meet bank norms  

Documents Required:  
• KYC: PAN Card, Identity Proof, Address Proof  
• Income Proof: Salary slips/Form 16/Bank statements (salaried); ITR/Bank statements/Business proof (self-employed)  
• Property Documents: Sale deed, allotment letter, building plan approvals, occupancy certificate (as applicable)  

Features:  
• Competitive floating rate from 8.75% p.a.  
• Fixed-rate option at 14% p.a.  
• Tenure up to 30 years and high-value financing up to ₹5 crore  
• Online eligibility calculator for quick check

🏦 AXIS BANK HOME LOAN – BALANCE TRANSFER / TOP-UP
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Facility to transfer existing home loan from another lender to Axis Bank and/or take a top-up loan for additional funds.

Interest Rate:  
• Starting from 8.75% p.a. (floating; for CIBIL 751+)  
• Typical Range: 8.75% – 12.80% p.a. (profile-dependent)  
• Fixed Rate: 14.00% p.a.  

Processing Fee:  
Up to 1% of the loan amount + GST (minimum ₹10,000)

Tenure:  
Up to 30 years (subject to age and policy)

Loan Amount Range:  
As per outstanding/valuation and eligibility; up to ₹5 crore (standard home loan maximum)

Eligibility:  
• Existing Borrower with another lender or Axis Bank  
• Good repayment track record  
• Credit score preferably 750+  
• Minimum Age: 21 years; maximum per standard norms  

Documents Required:  
• Existing Loan: Loan statements (6–12 months), NOC (if required)  
• KYC & Income Proof: As per standard home loan documentation  
• Property & Valuation: Existing property papers, approvals, title deed, survey  

Features:  
• Lower rate through balance transfer  
• Combine home loan with additional top-up funding  
• Quick online eligibility check

🏡 ASHA HOME LOAN (AFFORDABLE HOUSING)
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Affordable home loan for low-income individuals purchasing small/affordable homes; includes higher LTV and special repayment perks.

Interest Rate:  
• Salaried: 10.15% – 11.90% p.a.  
• Self-employed: 11.15% – 11.90% p.a.  
• Fixed Rate: 14.00% p.a.  

Processing Fee:  
Up to 1% of loan amount + GST (minimum ₹10,000) — may be waived/discounted under scheme.

Tenure:  
Up to 30 years (subject to age and policy)

Loan Amount Range:  
₹1 lakh – ₹30–35 lakh (approx; as per affordable housing norms)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Indian  
• Minimum Combined Income: ₹8,000–₹10,000 per month (low-income households)  
• Age: 21 years at start, up to 60/65 years at maturity  
• Other: Must qualify under affordable housing scheme norms (EWS/LIG categories or listed projects)

Documents Required:  
• KYC: PAN, Identity & Address proof  
• Income Proof: Salary slips, bank statements, or other income proof  
• Property: Sale deed, allotment letter, building plan, approvals, occupancy certificate  

Features:  
• Designed for affordable housing segment  
• Up to 90% Loan-to-Value (LTV) for eligible properties  
• EMI waiver benefits for consistent repayment
