🚗 NEW CAR LOAN
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Loan for purchase of brand-new cars (including SUVs and MUVs) with up to 100% on-road funding for eligible models.

Interest Rate:  
~8.75% – 11.80% p.a. (effective ROI range for new car loans)

Processing Fee:  
₹3,500 – ₹12,000 + documentation charges

Tenure:  
Up to 84 months (7 years) or as per bank policy

Loan Amount Range:  
Starting from ₹1 lakh; up to 100% of on-road price for eligible cars

Eligibility:  
• Minimum Age: 18 years  
• Maximum Age at Loan Maturity: 60 years (salaried)  
• Minimum Net Annual Salary: ₹4,00,000 (approved models) / ₹6,00,000 (others)  
• Self-employed Minimum Net Income: ₹3,50,000 – ₹6,00,000 p.a.  
• Stable income and good credit history (CIBIL ~750+ preferred)

Documents Required:  
• KYC: PAN, Identity & Address Proof  
• Income Proof (Salaried): Salary Slips, Form 16, Bank Statements  
• Income Proof (Self-Employed): ITR (last 2–3 years), Audited Financials, Bank Statements  
• Vehicle: Dealer Proforma Invoice/Quotation  

Features:  
• Up to 100% on-road funding  
• Online application with video KYC  
• Competitive rates for good profiles  

Sources:

🚘 PRE-OWNED / USED CAR LOAN
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Loan for purchase of used/pre-owned cars with shorter tenure and slightly higher interest rates.

Interest Rate:  
~13.55% – 15.80% p.a. (used car loans)

Processing Fee:  
₹6,000 or 1% of loan amount (whichever higher) + documentation charges

Tenure:  
Up to 60 months (5 years), subject to vehicle age

Loan Amount Range:  
From ₹1 lakh upwards; up to ~85–100% of car valuation (as per policy)

Eligibility:  
• Minimum Age: 21 years  
• Maximum Age: 60 years (salaried) / 65 years (self-employed)  
• Minimum Net Annual Income: ₹2,40,000 (salaried) / ₹2,00,000–₹3,50,000 (self-employed)  

Documents Required:  
• KYC: PAN, Identity & Address Proof  
• Income Proof: Salary slips, Bank Statements, ITR (for self-employed)  
• Vehicle: RC Copy, Insurance Certificate, Valuation Report  

Features:  
• Financing for used/pre-owned cars with simple process  
• Attractive offers for existing car loan customers  
• Faster approvals with minimal paperwork  

Sources:  
https://www.axisbank.com/retail/loans/car-loan/pre-owned-car-loan

🔁 CAR LOAN BALANCE TRANSFER & TOP-UP
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Facility to transfer existing car loan from another lender to Axis Bank or avail additional top-up on existing car loan.

Interest Rate:  
~12.20% – 16.90% p.a. (balance transfer / top-up)

Processing Fee:  
Around 1% of loan amount + documentation charges

Tenure:  
Up to 60 months (subject to vehicle age and bank policy)

Loan Amount Range:  
Based on outstanding amount, valuation, and eligibility

Eligibility:  
• Existing car loan with another lender or Axis Bank  
• Vehicle meets bank’s valuation and age norms  
• Satisfactory repayment history required  

Documents Required:  
• Existing Loan: Last 6–12 months statements, NOC from previous lender  
• KYC: PAN, Identity & Address Proof  
• Vehicle: RC Book, Insurance Copy  

Features:  
• Reduce EMI or interest through transfer  
• Additional top-up funds for existing car owners  
• Simplified approval for existing customers
