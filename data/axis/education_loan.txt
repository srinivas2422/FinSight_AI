🎓 EDUCATION LOAN – INDIA / ABROAD
-----------------------------------------
Description:  
Education loan for full-time eligible courses in India or abroad covering tuition, living expenses, travel, and other approved academic costs.

Interest Rate:  
• Starting from ≈9.99% p.a.  
• Secured: ≈9.99% – 11% p.a.  
• Unsecured: ≈10.75% – 13% p.a.  
Note: Depends on loan amount, institution, course, collateral/guarantor, and applicant profile.

Processing Fee:  
Up to 2% of loan amount + GST (as per bank site)

Tenure:  
Up to 15 years (including course + repayment period)

Loan Amount Range:  
From ₹50,000 upward; higher limits possible for international studies (subject to eligibility)

Eligibility:  
• Nationality: Indian citizen  
• Academics: Minimum 50% in HSC (10+2) or previous qualifying exam  
• Admission: Must have confirmed admission in recognised institution (India or abroad)  
• Age: Minimum 16 years  
• Co-applicant: Parent/guardian with regular income required  

Documents Required:  
• KYC: PAN, Identity & Address proof (student & co-applicant)  
• Admission Proof: Offer/Admission letter, Fee structure/cost estimate  
• Income Proof (Co-applicant): Salary slips/bank statements/ITR  
• Academic Proof: Mark-sheets (10+2 and prior degrees)

Features:  
• Covers up to 100% of approved educational expenses  
• Moratorium covering course duration + grace period  
• Tax benefit under Section 80E for interest paid
