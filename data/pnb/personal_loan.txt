💼 PERSONAL LOAN SCHEME FOR PUBLIC
-----------------------------------
Description:  
General personal loan scheme for salaried and other eligible individuals to meet personal needs such as travel, education, medical, or family expenses.

Interest Rate:  
Approx 11.15% – 16.70% p.a. (depending on profile)

Processing Fee:  
Up to 1.00% of the loan amount (as per bank policy)

Tenure:  
Up to 60 months (5 years) or as per bank policy for general salaried segment

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: ₹50,000  
• Maximum: 20× net monthly income or up to ₹10 lakh (approx)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Indian National  
• Minimum Age: 21 years  
• Maximum Age: Up to 60 years or age of retirement at loan maturity

Documents Required:  
• Completed loan application form with photographs  
• PAN / Aadhaar / Driving Licence  
• Proof of Residence (utility bill / Aadhaar)  
• Last 6 months’ salary slips (for salaried)  
• Bank account statement showing salary credits

-----------------------------------
⚕️ PNB DOCTOR’S DELIGHT – PERSONAL LOAN FOR DOCTORS
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Special personal loan scheme for professionally qualified doctors (MBBS/BDS & above) to meet professional or personal financial needs.

Interest Rate:  
Approx 9.95% p.a.

Processing Fee:  
As per bank policy (not publicly specified)

Tenure:  
Up to 84 months (7 years) for eligible doctors

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: ₹2,00,000  
• Maximum: Up to ₹15 lakh or 20× monthly income

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Indian National  
• Minimum Age: 18 years (must hold professional qualification)  
• Maximum Age: As per retirement/loan maturity policy

Documents Required:  
• Completed application form with photographs  
• Proof of professional qualification (MBBS/BDS or higher)  
• Valid registration/licence as a practising doctor  
• Income Tax returns for the past 2 years  
• Bank account statements  
• Proof of identity & residence

👴 PERSONAL LOAN SCHEME FOR PENSIONERS
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Loan for pensioners drawing pension through PNB to meet personal or medical needs.

Interest Rate:  
Approx 11.75% p.a. (floating) or ~12.75% p.a. (fixed)

Processing Fee:  
Nil (as per official listing)

Tenure:  
Up to 60 months or loan maturity till age 78 years (whichever earlier)

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: ₹25,000  
• Maximum:  
  - Age up to 70: ₹10 lakh or 18× net monthly pension (20× for defence pensioners)  
  - Age 70–75: ₹7.50 lakh or 18× net pension  
  - Above 75: ₹5.00 lakh or net pension for 12 months, whichever lower

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Indian National (pensioners drawing pension via PNB)  
• Minimum Age: 60 years (approx)  
• Maximum Age: 78 years at loan maturity

Documents Required:  
• Completed application form  
• Pension Payment Order / pension passbook  
• Bank statement showing pension credits  
• Proof of identity and address  
• Guarantor/third-party guarantee (as per bank policy)

-----------------------------------
🏡 PNB BAGHBAN – SCHEME FOR SENIOR CITIZENS (REVERSE MORTGAGE)
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Loan scheme for senior citizens owning a residential house or flat to supplement their monthly income or meet other personal needs.

Interest Rate:  
Approx 10.55% p.a.

Processing Fee:  
As per bank policy (not publicly disclosed)

Tenure:  
Up to 20 years (subject to property value and borrower’s age)

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: As per policy  
• Maximum: Up to ₹1 crore (approx)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Indian National senior citizens owning residential property  
• Minimum Age: 60 years  
• Maximum Age: Up to 78 years at loan maturity (approx)

Documents Required:  
• Completed application form with photographs  
• Proof of residence and identity  
• Proof of ownership of residential property  
• Property valuation report (if required)  
• Bank statements and income details of borrower/spouse

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------
Q: What is the interest rate for PNB personal loans?  
A: Interest rates vary by scheme and borrower type. Public scheme: 11.15%–16.70% p.a., Doctors: ~9.95% p.a., Pensioners: ~11.75% p.a.

Q: Is there a processing fee for PNB personal loans?  
A: Yes, up to 1% of loan amount for general loans. Pensioner loans have Nil processing fee.

Q: What is the maximum tenure for PNB personal loans?  
A: General: up to 5 years; Doctors: up to 7 years; Pensioners: up to 5 years; Senior citizens: up to 20 years (reverse mortgage).

Q: Can pensioners apply for personal loans?  
A: Yes, PNB offers a dedicated pensioner personal loan scheme.

Q: What is the minimum loan amount under PNB personal loans?  
A: Minimum ₹25,000 for pensioners and ₹50,000 for the general public.
