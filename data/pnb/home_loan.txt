🏠 PNB HOUSING LOAN FOR PUBLIC (General Purchase / Construction / Repair)
-----------------------------------
Description:  
A housing loan for the general public to purchase, construct, extend, or repair residential property.

Interest Rate:  
Starting from ~7.50% p.a. onwards (floating rate)

Processing Fee:  
0.35% of the loan amount (Min ₹2,500; Max ₹15,000) + GST

Tenure:  
Up to 30 years or up to maximum eligible age as per bank policy

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: Need-based (subject to bank norms)  
• Maximum: Up to ₹5 crore (for eligible cases)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian / Joint applicants allowed  
• Minimum Age: 18 years  
• Maximum Age: Up to 70 years or retirement (whichever is earlier)

Documents Required:  
• Completed Home Loan application form with photographs  
• Proof of Identity (PAN / Aadhaar / Passport / Driving License)  
• Proof of Residence (Utility Bill / Aadhaar / Passport)  
• Income Proof: Salary slips / employer certificate (for salaried); business financials (for self-employed)  
• Property Documents (Agreement for Sale, approved plan, occupancy certificate if ready-built, etc.)

🏠 PNB GEN-NEXT HOUSING LOAN (For Salaried Professionals under 40 Years)
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Home loan scheme specially crafted for younger salaried or professional applicants (Gen-Next) with flexible tenure and eligibility features.

Interest Rate:  
Starting from ~7.50% p.a. (floating rate, subject to applicant profile)

Processing Fee:  
0.35% of loan amount (Min/Max as per bank) + GST

Tenure:  
Up to 30 years (subject to maximum age at loan maturity)

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: As per bank policy for Gen-Next (around ₹10 lakh upward)  
• Maximum: Need-based, depending on eligibility

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian (salaried professionals)  
• Minimum Age: 21 years  
• Maximum Age: 40 years (at time of application)

Documents Required:  
• Application form with photographs  
• PAN / Aadhaar / Driving License  
• Proof of employment (offer or joining letter)  
• Last 3–6 months’ salary slips  
• Employer ID or Service certificate  
• Property documents as applicable

Home loan scheme with special benefits and preferential interest rates for Government and PSU employees.

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------
Q: What is the current interest rate for PNB home loans?  
A: Interest rates start from around 7.50% p.a. for eligible borrowers, depending on credit score, loan amount, and profile.

Q: What processing fee is charged for PNB home loans?  
A: Approximately 0.35% of the loan amount (Min ₹2,500; Max ₹15,000) + GST.

Q: What is the maximum tenure available?  
A: Up to 30 years, or until the age of 70 years / retirement, whichever is earlier.

Q: Can I apply jointly with a co-applicant?  
A: Yes, PNB allows joint home loan applications. Co-applicant’s income may be clubbed for eligibility.

Q: Is pre-payment or foreclosure allowed?  
A: Yes, prepayment and part-closure are allowed. Floating rate home loans generally have no prepayment penalty.
