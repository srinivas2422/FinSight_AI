= PUNJAB NATIONAL BANK (PNB)
===================================

== EDUCATION LOANS ==

Bank Type: Public Sector Bank  
Headquarters: New Delhi, India  
Official Website: https://pnb.bank.in/  

-----------------------------------
🏦 PNB SARASWATI EDUCATION LOAN
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Provides financial support to meritorious students for pursuing higher education in India. Covers approved courses leading to Graduate, Post-Graduate, or PG Diplomas within India.

Interest Rate:  
Approx 10.00% – 10.75% p.a. (for domestic education)

Processing Fee:  
Nil (as per official/third-party listings)

Tenure:  
Up to 15 years (maximum repayment period)

Loan Amount Range:  
Need-based finance (subject to margin rules):  
• Up to ₹4 lakh – Nil margin  
• Above ₹4 lakh – 5% margin

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian  
• Minimum Age: 18 years  
• Maximum Age: Not specified  

Documents Required:  
• Completed loan application form with photographs  
• Offer/Admission letter from institution  
• Mark sheets / qualification certificates  
• Proof of identity & address of applicant and co-borrower (parent/guardian)  
• Income proof of parent/guardian (if applicable)  
• Property documents (if collateral required)  

-----------------------------------
🏦 PNB PRATIBHA EDUCATION LOAN
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Education Loan Scheme for premier institutes — covers full-time degree, diploma, and PG diploma courses in selected top-tier institutions.

Interest Rate:  
Approx 8.20% – 10.50% p.a. (as per official listing)

Processing Fee:  
Nil  

Tenure:  
Up to 15 years repayment period  

Loan Amount Range:  
Need-based finance, subject to eligibility and institute category  

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Resident Indian  
• Minimum Age: 18 years  
• Maximum Age: Not specified  

Documents Required:  
• Completed loan application form  
• Admission/Offer letter from selected institute  
• Mark sheets / previous qualification certificates  
• Proof of identity & address (applicant & co-applicant)  
• Income proof of parent/guardian  

-----------------------------------
🏦 PNB UDAAN EDUCATION LOAN
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Education loan scheme for students pursuing higher education abroad.

Interest Rate:  
Approx 10% – 11.25% p.a. (depending on collateral and course)

Processing Fee:  
1% of loan amount (minimum ₹10,000)

Tenure:  
Up to 15 years (after moratorium period)

Loan Amount Range:  
Loans for study abroad up to ₹1 crore (as per bank’s policy)

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Indian National  
• Minimum Age: 18 years  
• Maximum Age: Not specified  

Documents Required:  
• Completed application form with photographs  
• Offer/Admission letter from foreign institution  
• Fee structure and schedule of expenses  
• Proof of identity & address (applicant & co-applicant)  
• Collateral security documentation (if required)  

-----------------------------------
🏦 PNB PM CARES EDUCATION LOAN SCHEME
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Education loan scheme for children who lost parent(s) due to COVID-19 under PM CARES for Children — provides low-interest financial support for higher education in India.

Interest Rate:  
Approx 9.50% – 10.00% p.a.

Processing Fee:  
Nil (as per listing)

Tenure:  
Up to 15 years (typical for education loans)

Loan Amount Range:  
Need-based finance for professional or higher education in India for eligible PM CARES beneficiaries.

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Indian National (eligible child under PM CARES)  
• Minimum Age: 18 years  
• Maximum Age: Not specified  

Documents Required:  
• Completed application form  
• Eligibility documents under PM CARES for Children scheme  
• Admission/Offer letter for course  
• Marksheets / qualification certificates  
• Proof of identity & address (applicant & guardian)

📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------
Q: What is the interest rate for PNB education loans?  
A: Rates vary by scheme, institute category, and collateral.  
• PNB Saraswati: ~10.00%–10.75% p.a.  
• PNB Udaan (abroad): up to ~11.25% p.a.  
• PM Vidyalaxmi/other special schemes: from ~7.50% p.a.

Q: Is processing fee charged on PNB education loans?  
A: For domestic schemes — mostly Nil. For PNB Udaan (abroad) — 1% of loan amount (min ₹10,000).

Q: What collateral or margin is required?  
A:  
• Up to ₹4 lakh – No margin, no collateral.  
• Above ₹4 lakh – 5% margin and collateral/security may be required.

Q: What is the maximum repayment tenure?  
A:  
Up to 15 years (post moratorium period – course period + 6 to 12 months).
