= PUNJAB NATIONAL BANK (PNB)
===================================

== GOLD LOANS / ADVANCE AGAINST SOVEREIGN GOLD BONDS ==

Bank Type: Public Sector Bank  
Headquarters: New Delhi, India  
Official Website: https://pnb.bank.in/  

-----------------------------------
🏦 ADVANCE AGAINST GOLD JEWELLERY / ORNAMENTS
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Loan granted by PNB against the security of pledged gold jewellery or ornaments for personal and non-agricultural purposes.

Interest Rate:  
Approx 9.25% p.a. (indicative rate for jewellery/ornaments)

Processing Fee:  
0.30% of the loan amount or ₹500 + GST, whichever is higher

Tenure:  
• Up to 12 months for bullet or demand loans  
• Up to 36 months for EMI-based term loans

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: ₹25,000 (approx)  
• Maximum: Up to ₹25 lakh (approx) against jewellery/ornaments

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Indian National  
• Minimum Age: 18 years  
• Maximum Age: Not specified  

Documents Required:  
• Completed application form with photographs  
• Proof of identity (PAN / Aadhaar / Passport / Driving License)  
• Proof of residence (Utility Bill / Aadhaar / Driving License)  
• Gold jewellery or ornaments to be pledged (typically 18–22 carat) with purity documentation  
• Bank account details for disbursement  

-----------------------------------
🏦 ADVANCE AGAINST SOVEREIGN GOLD BONDS (SGB)
-----------------------------------
Description:  
Loan granted by PNB against Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs) held in dematerialised form for personal or non-agricultural purposes.

Interest Rate:  
Approx 8.35% p.a. (indicative rate for SGB loans)

Processing Fee:  
Nil (for SGB advances, as per available listings)

Tenure:  
Up to 8 years or till date of maturity of the Sovereign Gold Bond, whichever is earlier

Loan Amount Range:  
• Minimum: ₹50,000 (approx)  
• Maximum: Need-based, subject to bank policy

Eligibility:  
• Resident Type: Indian National (Individuals / Trusts / HUFs as per SGB norms)  
• Minimum Age: 18 years  
• Maximum Age: Not specified  

Documents Required:  
• Completed loan application form with photographs  
• Sovereign Gold Bond certificate / demat holding proof  
• Proof of identity and address of borrower  
• Bank account details for disbursement  

-----------------------------------
📘 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
-----------------------------------
Q: What is the interest rate for PNB Gold Loans?  
A: For jewellery/ornaments, around 9.25% p.a.; for loans against SGBs, around 8.35% p.a. (indicative, subject to bank policy).

Q: What processing fee is charged on PNB Gold Loans?  
A: 0.30% of the loan amount or ₹500 + GST (whichever is higher) for jewellery/ornaments; Nil for SGB-based loans.

Q: What is the maximum tenure for a PNB Gold Loan?  
A: Jewellery/ornaments – up to 12 to 36 months; SGB loans – up to 8 years or till the bond matures.

Q: Can I take a loan against Sovereign Gold Bonds at PNB?  
A: Yes, PNB offers advances against SGBs held in demat form, subject to margin requirements and internal policy.

Q: What type of gold is accepted for the jewellery/ornaments loan?  
A: Gold jewellery and ornaments (typically 18–22 carat) are accepted; purity and LTV ratio are verified by the bank.
